## Detector research policy

Research reviewed includes ACL / NAACL studies showing that machine-generated-text detectors can degrade badly across unseen domains, models, adversarial edits and lightly AI-polished human text. Therefore:

- do not state authorship from style;
- do not identify a model from prose patterns;
- do not use a detector score as evidence of misconduct;
- describe observable signals and keep provenance separate.

## Art of Reduction

The owner-provided `art-of-reduction` skill is the method backbone: inspect before editing, use R0→R4, preserve evidence/accessibility/voice, and stop when another removal harms the intended outcome.
